# Product Philosophy

I believe strong products are built by combining:

**Customer Problems + Data + Experimentation + Technology + Business Impact**

My approach is centered around:

1. **Understand the problem**  
   Validate customer and business problems before jumping into solutions.

2. **Define the right product outcome**  
   Establish measurable goals and success metrics.

3. **Prioritize ruthlessly**  
   Focus engineering and business capacity on the highest-impact opportunities.

4. **Build with cross-functional teams**  
   Work closely with Engineering, Design, Data, QA and Business teams.

5. **Experiment and learn**  
   Use data, A/B testing and user feedback to continuously improve the product.

6. **Measure business impact**  
   Track adoption, engagement, efficiency, revenue and customer outcomes.
